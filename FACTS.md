## Inferences and choices (marked on screen where shown)

- "A hundred and fourteen years" = 1893 → 2007.
- The then-and-now pair of the courthouse square is marked "≈ same view".
- Dot matrix: 1,800 dots (AP's local job losses), the last 550 (the final shift) in red. These are two different counts on one picture, labelled as such.
- AP's peak (4,000) and CBS's (more than 3,000) disagree; the film uses CBS's, which also gives the share of the town.
- The Multi-Motor's year: 1914 (three sources) over 1915 (Farm Collector).
- Gyrafoam spelling as Maytag used it (Gas Engine Magazine); some sources write "Gyrofoam".
- The lonely repairman appears only in narration; no advertising footage or stills are used.
